1. Problem & scope

ClimaGuard estimates regional disease risk (Low / Moderate / High) from weather, air quality, location and socioeconomic context. It is a nowcast for a population, built on weekly country-level data — it does not know anything about an individual's health and is not a diagnosis.

Input

Weekly temperature, rainfall, heat-wave days, PM2.5, AQI, plus country context.

Output

An overall risk class and five disease scores (respiratory, vector-borne, heat, waterborne, cardiovascular).

Use

Public-health style guidance from a transparent rule engine — not clinical advice.

5. Feature engineering & selection

Engineered feature families (per country, weekly)
How they are built: rolling mean / sum / max over 4, 8 and 12 weeks; lags of 1, 2, 4 and 8 weeks (shifted so the current week never leaks); sin/cos of week and month so week 52 sits next to week 1; products such as temperature × PM2.5; one-hot dummies for country, region, income and climate zone.

    6. Chronological split

    Why not a random split? Lag and rolling features copy information across neighbouring weeks. With a random split, a test week's own values would appear inside the training rows' lag and rolling windows, so the model would effectively see the future and the scores would be inflated. Splitting by date keeps every test week strictly after everything the model was trained or tuned on.

    12. Limitations

    • Missing variables: no humidity, elevation, UV, wind or confirmed case counts in the training data.
    • Country level: trained on one weekly series per country; the Bangladesh division map only varies local weather.
    • Weekly resolution: the models see 7-day aggregates, not daily or hourly conditions.
    • Dataset scales: the dataset's temperature is not real °C, so live temperature is quantile-mapped.
    • Frozen inputs: past disease counts (autoregressive features) stop at the dataset's last week.
    • Cardiovascular: no predictive skill (test R² ≈ 0).
    • Waterborne leakage: its rolling-window features include the current week's case count, so its R² is optimistic.
    • Prototype only — not a clinical tool. Do not use it for medical decisions.
